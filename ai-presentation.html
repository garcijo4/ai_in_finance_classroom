AI Powered Presentation

A hands-on tutorial demonstrating how AI can make finance teaching faster, more engaging, and more effective.

Overview

This tutorial walks through practical, real-time demonstrations of how finance faculty can integrate generative AI into their teaching workflows — from course preparation and content creation to student assessment and feedback. Rather than discussing AI in the abstract, this session shows AI in action on tasks that finance instructors face every week.

The presentation is designed as a companion to the Top 10 AI Applications for Finance Classroom Instruction guide, bringing several of those applications to life through live examples. Attendees will see how AI tools can be used to draft case materials, generate exam questions anchored to real financial data, build interactive simulations, and streamline the feedback cycle — all while maintaining the rigor and accuracy that finance instruction demands.

What You Will Learn

  • Rapid content creation — How to generate and customize finance case studies, problem sets, and exam questions in minutes rather than hours.
  • Live AI demonstrations — Step-by-step examples of AI-assisted teaching tasks, from building a DCF model walkthrough to creating role-play scenarios for client-facing communication.
  • Quality assurance — Strategies for verifying AI-generated financial content and incorporating human-in-the-loop review to maintain accuracy.
  • Immediate applicability — Every technique demonstrated can be replicated using free AI tools with no coding or IT infrastructure required.

Who Is This For?

This tutorial is designed for finance faculty who want to see concrete examples of AI in a finance classroom context before committing to adoption. Whether you are new to AI tools or already experimenting, the session emphasizes practical workflows you can take back to your next class.

Conference Context

This tutorial is part of the Southwestern Finance Association (SWFA) Annual Meeting, March 2026. It accompanies the presentation on the top 10 AI applications for finance instruction and is designed for faculty at regional universities and liberal arts institutions who want to adopt AI without requiring institutional infrastructure or dedicated IT support.
